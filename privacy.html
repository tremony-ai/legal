Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Tremony, Inc. (“Tremony”, “we”) collects through the Tremony production planning service and this website, how we use it, who processes it on our behalf, how long we keep it, and how you can exercise your rights, including asking us to delete your data.

1. Who we are

Tremony, Inc. is responsible for the personal data described in this policy. For any privacy question or request, contact us at info@tremony.com.

2. Data we collect

  • Dashboard accounts. Details of the people who use the Tremony dashboard, such as name, work email address, company and login credentials.
  • Factory production and ERP data. Data a customer connects to Tremony from its ERP and production systems, such as orders, due dates, inventory, work centers, machine status and production output.
  • WhatsApp field reports. Factory staff can report events on the shop floor (for example a machine stop) by sending a WhatsApp message. We do not store the message text or the sender’s phone number in our database. The message is processed only to extract the report; we store only the confirmed, structured information (such as the machine, the type of event and the time) together with the worker’s code.
  • Website enquiries. If you contact us by email or book a call through the scheduling form on this website, we receive the details you provide, such as your name, email address and company.

3. How we use the data

  • To provide and operate the service: mirroring production data, building and updating production plans, and showing alerts and answers in the dashboard.
  • To manage dashboard accounts and keep the service secure.
  • To respond to enquiries and arrange calls.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who processes the data for us

We use the following service providers to run Tremony. They process data on our behalf and only for these purposes.

Some of these providers may process data outside your country. Where that happens, we rely on the safeguards they offer for international transfers.

5. How long we keep data

  • WhatsApp field reports. Working data used while a report is being created and confirmed (open conversations, message identifiers, pending reports) is deleted after 30 days. Message text and sender phone numbers are never stored. Once a report is confirmed, its structured content (for example the machine, the reason or the quantity, and the worker code, never a phone number) becomes part of the factory’s production records and is kept for as long as those records are needed for the service, then deleted when the customer relationship ends or on request.
  • Other data is kept for as long as it is needed to provide the service to the customer, and is deleted when the customer relationship ends or when you ask us to delete it, unless we must keep it by law.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you are, you have rights under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). These include the right to know whether we process your data, to access it, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to processing, to receive it in a portable format, and to complain to a data protection authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK).

7. How to request deletion of your data

  1. Email info@tremony.com with the subject “Data deletion request”.
  2. Tell us who you are: the email address of your dashboard account, or, if you sent WhatsApp field reports, your company name and worker code.
  3. We will confirm your request and delete your data within 30 days, and let you know when it is done.

You can use the same address for any other request about your data.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
